An intro to virtual worlds

Virtual worlds are creator-led economies. Players build experiences, items and communities around the brands they love, with or without permission. Attention that brands used to buy on other channels now lives inside Roblox, Fortnite and their peers, and players pay it willingly. Brands are committing: activity is maturing from one-off experiments into always-on presence. GEEIQ centralises public and partner data across Roblox, Fortnite and ZEPETO, so performance can be read on visits, repeat play and item sales rather than reach alone.

How brands enter virtual worlds

Creator-made first

Most brands appear in virtual worlds before they arrive. Players build the items, shops and roleplay worlds themselves, and that activity sets the reference point for how the brand is understood in-platform.

Presence, then permanence

The brands seeing compounding returns treat virtual worlds as an always-on channel rather than a campaign. One-off activations spike and fade; sustained presence accrues an audience.

Measure what players do

Visits, repeat play and item ownership say more than impressions. The platforms expose real behavioural data, so performance can be judged on engagement rather than reach alone.
