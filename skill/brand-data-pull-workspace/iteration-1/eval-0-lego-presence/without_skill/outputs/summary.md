# LEGO on Roblox: headline numbers

Data pulled 23 July 2026 from the Roblox APIs (games, catalog, groups/users) plus press coverage.

## The short version

LEGO has no owned presence on Roblox. No verified LEGO profile or group, no LEGO-published experience, and zero official LEGO avatar items in the marketplace. Everything LEGO-branded in the catalog is fan-made UGC from unverified creators, mostly single-digit favourites.

What LEGO does have is a partnership strategy: since April 2025 it has run at least 6 in-game activations inside some of the platform's biggest third-party titles, and one is live right now.

## Headline numbers

- 0 owned experiences, 0 verified profiles, 0 official marketplace items
- 6 partner-game activations since April 2025
- 20.8B combined lifetime visits across the six host titles
- ~129K combined concurrent players in those titles right now
- 149M combined favourites across the host titles
- 1 activation live today: LEGO NINJAGO in Dragon Adventures (10 Jul - 14 Aug 2026). The game is currently retitled "[LEGO] Dragon Adventures" in search.

## Caveats

- Visits, CCU and favourites are lifetime/current figures for the host games, not event-attributable numbers. Roblox does not expose event-level engagement publicly.
- Rewards from these events are in-experience unlocks (minifigure characters, dragons, cosmetics), not marketplace avatar items, which is why the official item count is zero.
- The Dragon Adventures blog calls this a returning partnership, so there may be a prior NINJAGO event in that title not captured above.
- IP-adjacent note: LEGO's flagship metaverse bet remains LEGO Fortnite with Epic; on Roblox it works through partner activations, with NINJAGO as the lead IP (4 of 6 activations).
