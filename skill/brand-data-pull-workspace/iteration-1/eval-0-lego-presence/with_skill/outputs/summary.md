Here's what LEGO's Roblox presence looks like from today's pull (snapshot 23 July 2026, top search results rather than a census).

**Headline: no official LEGO presence in the results.** Nothing in the marketplace or experience results was created by a LEGO account. Everything LEGO-branded we found is player-made and unofficial, with no affiliation to the brand.

**Items (marketplace)**
- We found 58 creator-made LEGO-named items from 34 distinct creators.
- Most are cheap avatar accessories and clothing: LEGO-style keychains, LEGO Sonic and Batman gear, brick-print suits, typically 5 to 175 Robux.
- Most favourited: "Marvel Lego Spiderman Keychain" by Xottic Cartoons, 1,616 favourites.
- Excluded 15 unrelated rows (mostly "[Content Deleted]" listings that no longer carry a name).

**Experiences**
- Of 118 experience results, only 1 actually carried the LEGO name: "[LEGO] Dragon Adventures" by Sonar Studios (a verified creator, unaffiliated with LEGO). The game has 1.67bn lifetime visits, around 9,000 players in it at the time of the pull, and a 93% like ratio. One caveat: those visits belong to the game as a whole, and the [LEGO] tag in the title looks like a recent update or event, so the reach is the game's, and only the current theming references LEGO.
- The other 117 results were generic building and brick games with no LEGO term in the name or creator, so we excluded them as noise. No lookalikes trading on LEGO product-line names (Ninjago, Duplo, Technic) showed up in the results either.

**YouTube**: skipped, no API key set in this environment, so no video counts this time.

The story here is that players got there first, but thinly. Unlike brands such as adidas that run official verified accounts, LEGO has no official footprint in these results, and the fan-made layer is small and low-value: 58 cheap items and a single experience that borrowed the name. Worth noting that Roblox historically polices LEGO's trademarks, which likely suppresses both official and fan content.

Data: `roblox_catalog_items.csv` (73 rows) and `roblox_games.csv` (118 rows) in the output folder. Visits are lifetime totals; player counts are the moment of collection.
