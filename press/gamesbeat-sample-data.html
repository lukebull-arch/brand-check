Brand footprints in virtual worlds: sample data

GEEIQ · Prepared for GamesBeat · Snapshot 7 Aug 2026

Figures are from the top Roblox search results and the YouTube Data API on the snapshot date. Counts describe what we found in the top results, not platform totals. Creator-made content is fan-made and unofficial.

KFC

Players built KFC on Roblox without KFC. KFC's own activity went to Fortnite instead (KFC Island, live since Aug 2024).

70.3mcombined lifetime visits, top KFC experiences (all fan-made)
600fan-made KFC items found on the marketplace, search not exhausted
44.0mvisits to Escape KFC Obby alone (by MSL Games)
626.6mviews on the top KFC gaming videos we sampled on YouTube

Naruto

The IP giant. Fan demand at scale, no official presence found on Roblox, and this undercounts: the biggest anime games avoid the name entirely.

260.6mcombined lifetime visits across 6 fan-made Naruto experiences
146.3mvisits to Naruto War Tycoon alone
150fan-made Naruto items found, none official
360.5mviews on the top Naruto gaming videos we sampled on YouTube

Walmart

The commitment story. An official world live on Roblox, with a fan-made tail around it.

39.3mvisits to Walmart Discovered, Walmart's official experience

Gucci

The luxury pioneer. Official items persist on Roblox, but no live official Roblox world today: Gucci Town ended in June 2024 and its current official worlds run on ZEPETO.

12official Gucci items still live on the marketplace
138fan-made Gucci items alongside them
41.2kfavourites on Gucci Diamond-Framed Sunglasses (official era)
1.32bnviews on the top Gucci gaming videos we sampled on YouTube
For contrast: Louis Vuitton has no official Roblox presence and almost no fan footprint, 16 fan-made items and no experiences found.
